roadster.game: fly a roadster from Earth to Mars

A free 3D browser game. A two-seat roadster with hidden aero, ground-effect fans and cold-gas thrusters lifts off a night desert and climbs into orbit. Then fly the Mars Run: through ring gates and an asteroid field, from the launch gate above Earth to the arrival gate in Mars orbit.

How to play

W and S: thrust. A and D: steer. Up and down arrows: climb and dive. Shift: boost. Space: air-brake. Esc: pause. Touch controls on phones.

Vehicles

Street, Thrust and Flight variants, with a finish studio for paint colour, metallic, clearcoat and wheel finish.

Built by Aswini. An independent prototype, not affiliated with any automaker.
